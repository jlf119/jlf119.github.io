Mixing the senses

After seeing one of the symptoms of coronavirus was Anosmia (lack of sense of smell) I researched it more and found people with Anosmia often report worse quality of life than those who go blind. I had seen no resources to help for it so wanted to create a tool for people with Anosmia to experience smells through smell visualisation. It is inspired by the taste visualisation from the disney film Ratatouille. The webapp including animation is coded using html, css and javascript.
